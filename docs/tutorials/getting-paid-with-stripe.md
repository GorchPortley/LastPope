# Getting paid: setting up Stripe

SDLabs uses Stripe to take payments and send money to sellers. If you want to sell anything (a paid design, a kit, a used driver), you need a Stripe payout account connected to your SDLabs account.

You **don't** need this to add drivers, publish free designs or buy things.

## What Stripe is doing here

When someone buys from you, they pay SDLabs through Stripe's checkout. We then send your share to your own Stripe account, and Stripe pays it into your bank.

We never see your bank details or ID documents. You give those to Stripe directly during setup.

## 1. Set up your shop profile

In your dashboard, open **Marketplace → Selling & Payouts**.

[SCREENSHOT: the Selling & Payouts page before Stripe is connected]

Fill in your shop profile first:

- **Shop name**: shown on your listings and on your shop page.
- **Shop URL**: the address of your public shop page. Leave it empty and it's built from your shop name. Changing it later breaks old links, so pick one you're happy with.
- **About your shop**: a few lines about you and what you sell.
- **Country**: where you or your business are based. **Get this right, because it can't be changed after you connect Stripe.** Stripe fixes the country of an account when it's created.

Save the profile.

## 2. Connect Stripe

Click **Connect Stripe**. You're taken to Stripe's own setup pages. Stripe will ask for things like:

- Your name, date of birth and address
- Whether you're selling as an individual or a business
- The bank account to pay into
- Sometimes a photo of your ID

It usually takes five to ten minutes. When you finish, Stripe sends you back to SDLabs.

If you stop halfway, that's fine. The page shows **Setup not finished** and a **Continue Stripe setup** button that picks up where you left off.

[SCREENSHOT: the Payouts with Stripe card showing "Setup not finished"]

## 3. Wait for verification

After setup, the card shows **Pending verification**. Stripe checks your details, which normally takes a few minutes but can take a few days. We e-mail you when it's done.

If you think it should be done by now, click **Refresh status** to ask Stripe again.

When the card shows **Active**, you can publish paid designs and marketplace listings.

[SCREENSHOT: the Payouts with Stripe card showing "Active"]

## What you're paid, and when

**The platform fee** is 10% of the item price. It isn't taken from shipping: if you sell a kit for $200 with $20 shipping, the fee is $20 and you get $200.

**When the money moves** depends on what you sold:

- **Designs:** your share is sent as soon as the buyer has paid.
- **Physical items:** your share is sent 14 days after you mark the order as shipped. This protects buyers if a parcel goes missing or arrives broken.
- **If the buyer reports a problem**, the payout waits until it's sorted out. See [Buying designs and gear](/blog/guides/buying-on-sdlabs) for how problem reports work.

After that, Stripe pays your balance into your bank on its normal schedule, usually a few days later.

## Keeping track

- **Selling & Payouts** shows your totals: gross sales, fees, what's been paid out and what's still pending.
- **Earnings** has the detail: your Stripe balance, payouts on their way to your bank, which order each transfer was for, and what SDLabs is still holding and when it will be released.
- **Open Stripe dashboard** takes you to your own Stripe account, for bank details, tax documents and payout history.

## Common questions

**I moved country. Can I change it?** Not on the same Stripe account. Contact us and we'll sort it out.

**Do I need a business?** No. Stripe lets you sign up as an individual.

**Can I sell free designs without Stripe?** Yes. Stripe is only needed once you charge for something.
