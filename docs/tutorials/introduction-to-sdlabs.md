# Introduction to SDLabs

SDLabs is a place to find, share and sell loudspeaker designs, and to look up the drivers they're built from. It's run by a speaker builder, for speaker builders. Everything here is built around measurements: if a design is on SDLabs, you can see how it measures before you cut any wood.

This post walks through what's on the site and where to find it.

## The design database

**Designs** in the top menu lists every published speaker design: two-ways, three-ways, subwoofers, full-range builds, portables and the odd experiment. Filter by type and price, and sort by date or price.

Each design page has:

- **Photos** of the finished build.
- **The measured response.** The designer's measurement files are drawn as a frequency response chart, with one line per driver and the summed response. Switch lines on and off, and hover or drag a finger across the chart to read the level at any frequency. The −3 dB point and midband level are worked out for you.
- **Specs:** impedance, power handling, number of drivers and a rough build cost.
- **A summary** from the designer: what it is, who it's for, what it sounds like.
- **The drivers it uses,** each linked to its page in the driver database.
- **What you download,** listed by kind: enclosure drawings, crossover files, measurement files, and a build guide PDF.
- **Reviews** from people who built it, and **a forum discussion** for questions.

Many designs are free. Some are paid, which is how designers get something back for the months that go into a good speaker. On a paid design you can see the summary, the response, the specs and which drivers it uses before you buy. The full description, the crossover and band details, box volumes, the parts list and the files come with the plans.

Downloads come as a ZIP with everything the designer uploaded. When a designer updates a design, you get the new version too, at no extra cost. The build guide PDF has your name in the footer; please don't pass paid plans around.

Everything you've downloaded or bought is in **My Library** in your dashboard.

## The driver database

**Drivers** lists the drivers used in designs on the site. Each driver page has the manufacturer's information: the datasheet Thiele/Small parameters with units, the manufacturer's response measurements drawn on the same chart as designs, photos, and the manufacturer's files to download for free.

A driver page also shows **every design that uses it**. That's often the quickest way to find a proven design for a driver you already own, or to see how a driver behaves in real builds.

Drivers marked **Official** were added by the manufacturer.

## Publishing your own design

If you've built something worth sharing, publish it. A design can be free or paid, and you can save it as a draft and come back to it as often as you like. Your work is saved as you go, so closing the form by accident doesn't lose anything.

You'll need every driver in your design to be in the driver database first. If one is missing, add it. It only takes a few minutes and anyone can do it.

- [Adding a driver to the database](/blog/guides/adding-a-driver)
- [Publishing a speaker design](/blog/guides/publishing-a-design)

Designers who know CSS can style their own description, with headings, lists and tables in their own look. It's optional, in the Advanced section of the design form.

## The marketplace and the SDLabs store

**Marketplace** in the top menu is where builders sell to each other:

- **Kits:** the parts to build a design, often from the designer.
- **Finished speakers:** completed builds.
- **Used gear:** drivers, crossovers, amps and test gear.

Payments go through Stripe. Sellers are paid after the item has shipped, so buyers are covered if something goes wrong. If something arrives broken or never turns up, you can report a problem from your order, and we step in if you and the seller can't sort it out.

**New drivers** are sold by SDLabs directly, from each driver's page. Look for "Sold by SDLabs" next to the price.

## Your dashboard

Once you're signed in, the **Dashboard** button in the header takes you to everything that's yours:

- **My Designs** and **My Drivers**: what you've published, and drafts you're still working on.
- **My Library** and **My Orders**: what you've downloaded or bought.
- **My Listings**, **Sales** and **Selling & Payouts**, if you sell in the marketplace.
- **Chat and Messages**: talk to other members directly.

## Your profile

Every member has a public profile with their designs, drivers and listings. If you sell, you also get a shop page you can link to from forums and social media.

## The forum and the Soap Box

Every design and driver has its own discussion thread in the **Forum**, linked from its page. It's the place for build questions, measurement comparisons and modifications. The forum also has general boards for anything speaker-related.

The **Soap Box** is the site blog, where you're reading this. It has news, guides and build stories.

## For manufacturers

If you make drivers, get in touch and we'll mark your account as a manufacturer. Drivers you add are shown as official. You can also download a spreadsheet of what designers measured for each of your drivers in their builds: the band it played, the box volume and their measured parameters. It's a good way to see how your drivers behave in real speakers.

## Questions

Ask in the forum, or message us from your dashboard. SDLabs is still young, and feedback goes straight into what gets built next.
