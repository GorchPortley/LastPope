# Buying designs and gear

You can get three kinds of things on SDLabs:

- **Designs** from other builders: free or paid plans, measurements and files
- **New drivers**, sold by SDLabs from each driver's page
- **Kits, finished speakers and used gear** from other users in the marketplace

You need an account and need to be logged in to buy or download anything.

## Free designs

A free design has no checkout. Click **Get for free** on the design page and it goes straight into your library.

## Paying for things

1. Click **Add to cart** on a design, a driver page or a marketplace listing.
2. Open the cart from the icon in the header. You can buy from several sellers in one go; the cart groups items by seller and shows each seller's shipping.
3. At checkout, choose a delivery address if anything needs shipping. Designs don't. You can save addresses under **Settings → Addresses**.
4. You pay on Stripe's checkout page. SDLabs never sees your card details.

A few things the cart won't let you do:

- Buy your own items
- Buy a design you already own
- Order more of something than the seller has in stock
- Order from a seller who doesn't ship to your country (the cart tells you which item is the problem)

## Your designs: My Library

Every design you've bought or downloaded for free is in **Marketplace → My Library**.

- **Download** gets the latest version.
- **All versions** lists every snapshot the designer has made. When they revise a design, you get the new version at no extra cost.

The PDF in the download has your name in the footer. Please don't pass paid designs around. Designers here are mostly hobbyists like you, and sales are what keep them publishing.

If you've bought a paid design, you can also see its private forum discussion, where buyers and the designer talk through the build.

## Physical orders: My Orders

**Marketplace → My Orders** lists everything you've ordered. Open an order to see each seller's part of it: what's been shipped, the tracking number and a **Track parcel** link.

When the parcel arrives and it's all fine, click **Confirm received**. It lets the seller know everything went well.

To ask a seller something before or after you buy, use **Contact seller**. It opens a chat with them.

## When something goes wrong

If an item didn't arrive, arrived damaged, isn't what was described, or a design download doesn't work, open the order and click **Report a problem**.

[SCREENSHOT: the Report a problem dialog]

Choose what's wrong, describe it, and add photos if they help (up to 5). The seller is notified and can reply, and may offer you:

- A full refund
- A partial refund
- A refund once you've sent the item back. If you accept this, you add the return tracking number, and the refund goes through once the seller has the item back.

You can follow the conversation in **Marketplace → Problem Reports**.

If you can't agree, or the seller doesn't answer, you can ask SDLabs to step in after 3 days, and we'll decide. Problems with items sold by SDLabs come straight to us.

**Time limits:**

- **Physical items:** up to 14 days after the seller marked the order shipped. If it never shipped, you can report it any time.
- **Designs:** up to 14 days after you paid.

The seller isn't paid for an order while a problem is open, so reporting it does protect you. Please give the seller a chance to answer first. Most problems turn out to be a slow courier.
